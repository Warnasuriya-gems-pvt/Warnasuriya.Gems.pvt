Our Story

Three Generations of Gemstone Excellence

Every stone in our collection is hand-selected by our master gemologists, cut to maximize brilliance, and certified by internationally recognized laboratories.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you ship internationally? +
Yes, we ship worldwide via FedEx and UPS with full insurance and tracking. All customs documentation is handled by our logistics team.
Are your gemstones treated or natural? +
We specialize in 100% natural, unheated gemstones. Any treated stones are clearly labeled as such with full disclosure. Most of our inventory comes with GIA or CGL certificates.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank wire transfers, PayPal, Wise (TransferWise), and major credit cards for purchases under $10,000. For larger transactions, we recommend wire transfer for security.
Can I request a video of a specific stone? +
Absolutely. We provide high-resolution videos in natural daylight, indoor lighting, and shade upon request. We want you to see exactly what you're buying.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day inspection period from delivery. If the stone does not match the description or certificate, you may return it for a full refund minus shipping costs.
